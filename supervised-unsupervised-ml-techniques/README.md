# Supervised & Unsupervised ML Techniques

## Overview
Three machine learning projects on a telecommunications-style customer dataset, each answering a distinct business question about churn and revenue.

## 1. Classification -- gradient boosting
Gradient boosting classifier to predict whether a customer is likely to churn, using demographics, service usage, and account characteristics.
- `classification_gradient_boosting.py`

## 2. Clustering -- k-means segmentation
K-means clustering (tenure, monthly charge, usage, age, income, and survey scores) to segment customers into groups -- e.g., long-tenure/high-satisfaction vs. new/lower-satisfaction -- to support targeted retention strategy.
- `clustering_kmeans.py`

## 3. Time series -- ARIMA forecasting
ARIMA model to forecast daily revenue 30 days out, including stationarity testing, ACF/PACF diagnostics, and trend/seasonal decomposition.
- `time_series_arima.py`, `time_series_arima.ipynb`

## Skills demonstrated
Gradient boosting classification, k-means clustering, ARIMA time-series forecasting, model evaluation, translating model output into business recommendations.
