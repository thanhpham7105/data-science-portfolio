# Data Cleaning, Exploration & Market Basket Analysis

## Overview
Three data preparation and exploration projects, each on a different business dataset.

## 1. Data profiling & cleaning
Profiled a ~10,000-row employee dataset (16 variables) ahead of a retention-prediction effort: classified every variable by type (quantitative/qualitative, continuous/discrete/nominal/ordinal) and documented data-quality issues.
- `data_profiling_and_cleaning.py`

## 2. Exploratory data analysis
Univariate and bivariate statistical analysis of insurance-style billing data (age, charges, smoker status, region), including hypothesis testing on the relationship between smoking status and charges.
- `exploratory_data_analysis.py`

## 3. Market basket analysis
Applied the Apriori algorithm to retail transaction data to identify frequently co-purchased product categories, generating association rules (support/confidence/lift) to inform cross-sell and promotion strategy.
- `market_basket_analysis.py`

## Skills demonstrated
Data profiling & type classification, univariate/bivariate visualization, hypothesis testing, association-rule mining (Apriori), business-oriented interpretation of statistical results.
